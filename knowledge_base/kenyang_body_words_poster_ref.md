# Body words from a published poster (unconfirmed)

Source: the poster Peti Peti Chi Nekor, a published work. These are its spellings,
transcribed as printed, singular only. They are a book's forms, not the speaker's own:
none of them has been said aloud or confirmed by the speaker.

Where the speaker's verified files already have the word, the speaker's form is the
one taught, and the poster's form is listed in the verified file as a book spelling
of it: face, ear, hand, leg, finger, head and eye. Those seven are not repeated here.

The compounds are given exactly as the poster writes them, on the poster's own roots
(ekak, awuor, amoh). They are not rebuilt on the speaker's forms.

## Greetings and replies

From the same poster, Peti Peti Chi Nekor, filed 26 November 2026 ahead of its full
list. The poster's spellings, unconfirmed by a speaker. Each greeting is on one line
with the reply the poster gives for it; the poster calls each of these "reply" and gives
it no English of its own. Oki'ah and M'kaka are one entry on the poster, "Oki'ah /
M'kaka", for thank you.
