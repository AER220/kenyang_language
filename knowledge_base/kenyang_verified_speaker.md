## Verbs & phrases
- ndɔk — I go.
  - ndɔk? (rising / questioning) — "Should I go?"
  - ndɔk! (exclamation) — a stronger, more urgent sense (e.g. said in a moment
    of trouble or decision).
  - Note: ndɔk already carries the "I" (n-) prefix, so it means specifically
    "I go," not a bare "go."
