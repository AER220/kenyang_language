# Kenyang texts, confirmed by a native speaker

Whole pieces of real Kenyang speech, each line with its meaning. Confirmed by me,
Agbor Edouard Ransome, a native Kenyang speaker from Bachou Ntai.

Connected speech teaches what single phrases cannot. A word list gives pieces; a
text shows how one idea joins to the next, how a command softens into a wish, and
how a speaker addresses the dead and the living in the same breath.

Everything in this file is correct Kenyang and can be taught as it stands.

---

# Text 1: A meeting at Arrey

What is said at a village meeting when the drink is poured and the ancestors are
called on.

## The first pouring

    Ghó ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ́ ɛ́chémtí.
    People are already gathered for the meeting

    Mántwó nɛ mmɛm bataŋ-bataŋ.
    let them come with strong drinks

    Kɛ mánáŋ bátwɔ nɛ mɔ́.
    but they have already come with it

    bɛ́ ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ ɛ́nkɔ pɛtii.
    so that this meeting will be a success

    Bɔ ɛ́tá, ghó ka bo abhɛ́n bátwɔ́
    Our forefathers, look at those who have already come

    bɛtɔkɔ́-bɛtɔk ndu ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ́n nɛ.
    from different villages because of this meeting

    Ghó awɔ́-ghɔ anɛ́ mmɛ nchí fyɛ́
    this is my left hand I am putting in

    Mánsɔt mmɛm amɛn mánnyú
    let them take these drinks and drink

    Ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ ɛ́ndɔ́k nɔ́kɔ́ pɛtii
    let this meeting be a success

## If someone brings trouble

    Mmu anɛ áfú ɛyi ɛtɔk,
    If somebody comes from his village

    ábhɔ́ŋ ndak nɛ ntɨ
    and has a problem with somebody

    kɛ yí bɛ́ ámbírɛ́ fa yí ɛ́nchɔ́ŋtí nnyɛ́n Are
    he plans in his heart to blackmail the name of Arrey

    bɛ́ ɛsɔŋɔri ɛnɛ ɛ́mfú chí fa Are
    that this problem should come from Arrey

    awɔ́-ghɔ ánsɔt nɔkɔ yí
    the left hand should take him

    Mámbɛp nɔkɔ yí bɛ́
    they will be asking him

    ɛtɔk Are kɛ ɛ́bhɔ́ŋ ɛsɔŋɔri?
    the village of Arrey hasn't a problem

    Ɔku na kɛ ɔpɔkɔ ɛsɔŋɔri ɔtwɔ́ fa nɛ yɔ́?
    what happened before you brought this problem to this village?

    Ghó awɔ́-nɛ́m aywɛsɛ́ sɛ́chí wɛn
    This is our right hand of those of us who are here

## The drinking begins

    Sɛ́twó bhɔ mmɛm mɛ́nyú
    We are going to start drinking

    Sɛ́nku sɛ́mbɛ́n babhɛ́n
    let us dance

    Sɛ́ndɛm kɛpɨ
    let us talk

    Mănto nɔkɔ manyɛp akwɛn
    You people should be pouring cold water

    Ndu nɛchɔkɔ ɛnɛsɛ́ nɛ́chɔ́kɔ́ fa nɛ́nɛ
    in the meeting we are gathered here

    mănáŋ ḿbú ányu amɛka mmɛm
    You have taken your own drink

    Bátwɔ́ bhɔ ɛchɛmɛ
    the meeting is going to start

    Ghó mbɔk mmɛm arɔp amɛn sɛ́twó nkwɔ́ nyú.
    some drinks have remained that we are going to drink also

## The closing words

    Sɛ́rɛm fa ndu ɛchɛmɛ bɛ́ sɛ́mbák ɛyɔŋ ɛ́mɔt.
    We are saying that in this meeting, we should be of one voice

    Yɛ̌ mpútí ákɛ́ bhák arɛ́.
    there should be no arguments

    Ntí ákɛ́ sáy mmú
    nobody should have any head ache

    nɛ awɔ́-nɛ́m ámbak nɔkɔ aywɛsɛ ndu ɛtɔk Are nɛ.
    Let our right hand be with us here in Arrey

# What Text 1 teaches

## The marker in front of the verb says who, and says should

A short marker joins to the front of the verb. It carries both the person and the
sense of *should* or *let*. These are the forms this text gives, each one with the
meaning it has here.

**Let them, they should**

    Mántwó              let them come
    Mánsɔt              let them take
    Mánnyú              let them drink
    Mámbɛp nɔkɔ yí      they will be asking him

**Let him, he should**

    ánsɔt nɔkɔ yí       the left hand should take him
    ámbak nɔkɔ          it should keep being
    ákɛ́ bhák            it should not be
    ámbírɛ́              he plans

**Let it, it should**

    ɛ́nkɔ pɛtii          it will be a success
    ɛ́ndɔ́k nɔ́kɔ́        let it be
    ɛ́mfú               it should come
    ɛ́bhɔ́ŋ              it has
    ɛ́nchɔ́ŋtí           to spoil, to blackmail

**Let us, we should**

    sɛ́mbák             we should be
    sɛ́chí              we are
    Sɛ́twó              we are going to
    Sɛ́ndɛm             let us talk
    Sɛ́nku sɛ́mbɛ́n      let us dance

**You people should**

    Mănto nɔkɔ          you people should be pouring

This joins the forms already in this file: bɔ́ mántɨ́knsɛm, they should repent, and
mmá amfoŋori, mother should forgive.

## náŋ: already, have done

    Kɛ mánáŋ bátwɔ nɛ mɔ́      but they have already come with it
    mănáŋ ḿbú ányu            you have already taken

This is the same naŋ already in this file, meaning to finish, and Ánáŋ, done.
Put on the front of a verb it says the thing is already done.

## Three ways of saying no

Each has its own job and they are not interchangeable.

    pú        not, in a plain statement       mme pú rong
    Kɛ́        do not, as a command            Kɛ́ dɔ́k
    á-kɛ́      should not                      ákɛ́ bhák

From the text:

    Yɛ̌ mpútí ákɛ́ bhák arɛ́       there should be no argument
    Ntí ákɛ́ sáy mmú              no one should have a headache

## ghó: here is, look

Ghó opens a line to point at something, the way a hand points.

    Ghó ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ́ ɛ́chémtí      look, this meeting is gathered
    Ghó ka bo abhɛ́n bátwɔ́       look at those who have come
    Ghó awɔ́-ghɔ anɛ́             here is this left hand
    Ghó mbɔk mmɛm arɔp           here is the drink that is left

## This changes shape with the noun

The word for *this* is not one word. It answers the noun it sits with.

    ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ́              this meeting
    bo abhɛ́n                these people
    mmɛm amɛn               these drinks
    Mmu anɛ                 the person who

## Saying it twice makes it stronger

    bataŋ-bataŋ             strong, of drink
    bɛtɔkɔ́-bɛtɔk            village after village, different villages

## ndu: in, and because of

    ndu ɛchɛmɛ ɛnɛ́n nɛ      because of this meeting
    Ndu nɛchɔkɔ ɛnɛsɛ́       in the meeting

With ndǔ already in this file for *as* and *during*, this word covers in, during,
as, and because of. The sense comes from what follows it.

## nɔkɔ keeps its place

nɔkɔ comes straight after the verb and means to keep doing. The text confirms it
with four different people:

    ánsɔt nɔkɔ yí           should keep taking him
    Mámbɛp nɔkɔ yí          keep asking him
    Mănto nɔkɔ manyɛp       keep pouring water
    ámbak nɔkɔ aywɛsɛ       should keep being with us

## yɛ̌: not a single one

    Yɛ̌ mpútí ákɛ́ bhák arɛ́       there should be no argument at all

With yɛ̌ mmu, no one, already in this file: yɛ̌ in front of a noun, with a negative
on the verb, says not a single one of these.

## Words this text confirms in use

    ɛchɛmɛ      meeting             nnyɛ́n       name
    mmɛm        drink, wine         ɛtɔk        village, town
    awɔ́-ghɔ     left hand           ntɨ         heart
    awɔ́-nɛ́m    right hand          ɛsɔŋɔri     trouble, problem
    ndak        problem             nɛ́nɛ        now
    ɛ́tá        father              kɛ          but
    bɛ́         that                ntí         head
