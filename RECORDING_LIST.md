# Recording list

The one place to work from. Ordered by what each recording unlocks, not by when it was
found. Updated 25 November 2026.

Not Kenyang to learn from: this file shows what the books say beside what you have
written, including forms nobody has confirmed. It must never go in `knowledge_base/`.

## What the marks mean

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Yours already** | Your own verified text has the word. Say it; there is nothing to invent. |
| **Book form to check** | Only a book or course has a form. You have not written it down. Say the word as you say it; the book's form is there to compare, not to read out. |
| **Not written anywhere** | No file of yours and no book has it. You supply the word as well as the voice. |
| **Not written: a sentence may hold it** | No word list has it, but one of your recorded sentences seems to (QUESTIONS 30). Say the word on its own, with its English. |

## How a recording gets counted

Two ways, and both count as yours on the pictures:

1. **A file.** Save it in `audio/`, add `{"file": "<name>", "kenyang": "<what you said>",
   "english": "<the English>"}` to `audio_words.json`, and run
   `python build_audio_manifest.py`.
2. **Your phone.** Record on nepem.org, approve it on the Review page, and tick **This
   recording is mine, in my own voice**. It reaches the pictures at the next publish (a
   push, or "Run workflow" on the Actions page). Without the tick it plays in the chat
   and on the site, but the pictures do not count it.

**Type the English exactly as in the "English, exactly" column**, the same words with
nothing added or dropped ("back of the head", not "back of head"; "eye", not "eyes").
Capitals and punctuation do not matter. A picture finds your recording in one of two
ways: by the spelling (if you write the word the same way the picture does, the label
becomes yours at once), or, if your spelling differs, by the English, character for
character. Then the picture shows your form and keeps the old one as "also written".
The publish log lists every one.
