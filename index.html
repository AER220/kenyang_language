It will not invent your language

Ask a general-purpose AI for Kenyang and it answers confidently and wrongly, because it has never seen the language. For a child learning, that is worse than no answer at all — the invented words are learned as real ones.

Everything here comes from Kenyang recovered from old dictionaries and grammars, then checked by a native speaker. When Nɛpɛm does not know something, it says so. Every word in these games has been confirmed.

Kenyang is spoken by about 65,000 people in the Southwest Region of Cameroon. It has never had an app, a course, or a page that teaches it. This is the first.
